MAIN OBSERVATION

The best-performing model differs between the two datasets.

For the IGP/IGI-specific dataset, SVR gives the best performance in both
training cases.

For the entire Delhi-region dataset, Gradient Boosting gives the best
performance in both training cases.

Accuracy (%) is not used because visibility prediction is a regression
problem. MAE, RMSE and R² are the appropriate primary evaluation metrics.
